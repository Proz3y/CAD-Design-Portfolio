
# Flange Coupling – Mechanical CAD Design

## Project Overview

A 3D CAD design of a flange coupling, a mechanical component used to connect two shafts and transmit rotational power.

## Design Objectives

- Model the coupling components.
- Explore flange geometry and bolt-hole arrangements.
- Develop a 3D representation of the assembly.
- Practise mechanical modelling and technical drafting.

## Software Used
- AutoCAD
